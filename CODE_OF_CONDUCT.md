# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberate intimidation
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be considered unprofessional in a collaborative environment

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject any contribution that violates this Code of Conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for sustained or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report violations by opening a private GitHub Security Advisory at:
`https://github.com/Anandb71/LoRA-jit/security/advisories/new`

All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with confidentiality.
