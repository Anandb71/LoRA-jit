# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Showing empathy and kindness
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
